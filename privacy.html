Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how Sage Lab ("we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile apps published on Google Play and the Apple App Store, and this website, sagelab.in. Sage Lab is a sole proprietorship based in Bhiwani, Haryana, India, registered as an MSME (Udyam No. UDYAM-HR-02-0056471).

An individual app may add details that apply only to it. Those details appear on the app's store listing and inside the app, and they take priority over this page where they differ.

Your documents and files

Files you open, scan, create or edit in our apps are processed on your device. We do not upload your documents to our servers, and we cannot see their contents. If an app offers a feature that needs to send a file elsewhere, such as cloud backup or sharing, it only does so when you choose to use that feature.

Our apps ask for permissions like camera or file access only when a feature needs them. You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings.

Information collected automatically

To keep our apps working, measure how they're used and pay for free versions through advertising, our apps use third-party services that may collect:

  • Device information, such as model, operating system version, language and screen size
  • Identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app instance IDs
  • Usage data, such as screens viewed, features used and session length
  • Crash logs and performance data
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (not precise GPS location)
  • Purchase and subscription status, if you buy something in an app

We do not collect your name, phone number or email address through our apps unless you contact us directly.

Advertising

Free versions of our apps may show ads served by Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised using your advertising ID. Where the law requires it, for example in the EEA and UK, we ask for your consent first. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings: on Android under Settings, Google, Ads; on iOS under Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or the App Store and renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel or manage them in your store account settings. Uninstalling an app does not cancel a subscription.

How we use information

  • To provide and maintain app features
  • To fix crashes and improve performance
  • To understand which features are used so we can improve them
  • To show ads and process purchases
  • To reply when you contact us
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data.

Data retention

Analytics and crash data are kept for the retention periods set by the services listed above, typically up to 14 months. Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and meet legal requirements.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent between our apps and the services above is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to raise a grievance. To make a request, email aman@sagelab.in with the app name and, if possible, the app instance ID shown in the app's settings. We respond within 30 days.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling an app removes the files and data it stored on your device. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, email us as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also let you know inside the affected apps.
