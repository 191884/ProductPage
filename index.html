A small studio with a wide range

Sage Lab is an independent app studio based in Bhiwani, Haryana. We design, build and publish our own mobile apps across many categories. When we spot an everyday problem that a well-made app can solve, we build one.

  • Fast on any phone

    Our apps open quickly and stay responsive on budget devices, not just flagships.

  • Your data stays yours

    We keep your data on your device wherever possible, and collect only what an app needs to work and to keep it free.

  • Clear about money

    When an app shows ads or offers a subscription, we say so plainly, and cancelling is always one tap away in your app store.
