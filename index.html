A small studio with a narrow focus

Sage Lab is an independent app studio based in Bhiwani, Haryana. We design, build and publish our own mobile apps, with a focus on tools people reach for every day to handle files and documents.

  • Fast on any phone

    Our apps open quickly and stay responsive on budget devices, not just flagships.

  • Your files stay yours

    Documents are processed on your device wherever possible. We collect only what an app needs to work and to keep it free.

  • Clear about money

    When an app shows ads or offers a subscription, we say so plainly, and cancelling is always one tap away in your app store.
